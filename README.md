# Piper Midihub Workbench

Piper is a browser-based editor for the Blokas Midihub. Turn a preset or device backup into JSON, edit it yourself or with an AI agent, and convert it back. You can play a preset away from your gear, watch MIDI events move through its pipes, and try it with a real controller or synth.

**[Open Piper in your browser](https://diab0lus.github.io/piper/piper.html)**

## Develop a preset with an AI agent

Open or create a preset or device setup in Piper, then choose **Download JSON**. Give the file to your agent and describe what you want the preset to do.

Open the returned JSON in Piper to hear or inspect the result. **Download Preset** saves the selected preset as an `.mhp`. For a device setup, **Download Everything** saves all eight presets, device settings, and shared tuning tables as an `.mhd`.
